Founder Pain Points & LP Communication
How we surface founder needs and connect them with LP expertise

Objective

Identify and document the most pressing, actionable challenges faced by our portfolio's founders, structure those insights into clear categories, and concisely communicate findings on a monthly basis to LPs so they may make introductions.

Four focus categories

Fundraising & Capital Access
Hiring & Talent
Product-Market Fit
Legal & Compliance

3-part workflow

1

Founder intake

Founders fill out a form with their company name and up to 3 pain points across the four categories. Guided prompts help them write in clear, useful language.

Open intake form ↗
2

Review & refine

Use AI to suggest more specific language for each entry. The team approves each pain point before it goes to LPs. This step also involves collecting updated pitch decks, job descriptions, or sales decks from founders.

3

Send to LPs

Email LPs the founder needs organized by category. For each need, an LP can indicate whether they can help and provide the name of someone to introduce. Relevant collateral (pitch deck, job description, sales deck) is included upfront.

Core activities

Surfacing pain points across all four categories — including securing appropriate collateral (job descriptions, pitch decks, sales decks) to support founders' specific needs.

Organizing pain points by theme, severity, and frequency. Communicating needs to LPs in a targeted, easy-to-respond-to format with all collateral included upfront.

Outputs

— Categorized summary of founder pain points with supporting context per company

— LP communication materials (email or form)

— List of action items following LP responses

What we're not doing

Presenting a long, exhaustive list of founder needs. Sending mass emails to LPs.
