Internal Process
Founder Pain Points & LP Communication
Identify and document the most pressing, actionable challenges faced by our portfolio founders, structure those insights into clear categories, and communicate findings monthly to LPs so they may make introductions.
Four focus categories
Fundraising & Capital Access
Hiring & Talent
Product-Market Fit
Legal & Compliance
3-part workflow
1
Founder intake
Founders fill out a form with their company name and up to 3 pain points across the four categories. Guided prompts help them write in clear, useful language.

Open intake form ↗
2
Review & refine
Use AI to suggest more specific language for each entry. The team approves each pain point before it goes to LPs. Collect updated pitch decks, job descriptions, or sales decks from founders at this stage.
3
Send to LPs
Email LPs the founder needs organized by category. For each need, an LP can indicate whether they can help and provide the name of someone to introduce. Relevant collateral is included upfront.
Outputs
— Categorized summary of founder pain points with supporting context per company
— LP communication materials (email or form)
— List of action items following LP responses
What we're not doing
Presenting a long, exhaustive list of founder needs. Sending mass emails to LPs.
